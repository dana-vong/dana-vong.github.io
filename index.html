Who I Am

Dana Vong is a Chinese American who has a Bachelor’s in Furniture Design and a Master’s in Interior Architecture. She is a multi-disciplinary artist and a jack of all trades. Dana loves to explore all forms of art but her favorites are painting and sculpting. In her free time, she likes to sketch furniture and the human form. The pieces she produces provokes thoughts and conversations surrounding the human experience and current events in America. She often draws from her feelings and experiences.
